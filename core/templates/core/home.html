{# -------------------------------------------- How the assistant helps #}

The assistant

It reads our catalogue, not the internet

  1. It recommends from real stock

    Ask for something calming, something iced, or something with no caffeine. It ranks the twenty-eight teas on the shelf and tells you why it chose them. It cannot suggest a tea we do not sell.

  2. It gives our brewing figures

    Temperature, weight, time and how many re-steeps, read straight from the same row the tea page shows you. If the two ever disagree, something is broken, and it is not the tea.

  3. It admits when it does not know

    For anything outside the shop's own notes it says so and opens a support ticket with a number, rather than guessing. It will also explain any error code this site shows you.
